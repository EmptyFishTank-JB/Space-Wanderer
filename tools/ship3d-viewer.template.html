      Before I build on it

      Name. "Halyard" is a working class name (the line that hoists a sail). Should the ship itself have a name the traveler uses in the logs?

      Silhouette. At 94 m it is roughly 60% of an Anaconda's length, sized for one person's life rather than a crew. Longer and leaner, or wider and heavier?

      New spaces. Should the washroom, the sample hold and the EVA lift become places the traveler actually uses, with their own cameras?
